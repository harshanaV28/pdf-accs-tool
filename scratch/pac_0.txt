Look at that color!
What’s your favorite color? When do you wear this 
color, and what does it mean to you? Color is an 
important part of fashion today, and it was also 
important in the past.
Today, the color green usually makes people think 
about nature. But from about 500–1300 CE, women 
in Europe wore green when they got married. They 
believed that green showed that they were ready to 
have children.
In the past, purple was the most difficult color to 
make. And it was very expensive. For these reasons, 
purple became the color of kings and queens. The 
famous Egyptian queen Cleopatra loved purple. She 
used it in her clothes and even on her ships to show 
her money and power.
When Spanish explorers arrived in Mexico in the 1500s, they saw a red color that was more beautiful 
than any red in Europe. The Aztec people used a small insect called a cochineal to make red for their 
clothes. Aztec women even painted this red on their teeth. It showed that they were important.
The next time you decide what to wear, think about the colors you’re choosing. What do these 
colors mean?

Questions
To form questions with be:  
Were you in the clothing store this morning? Was she in the shoe store?
All questions with other verbs begin with did. 
Didn’t you see the fashion show? Did they buy new clothes?
Some verbs in the simple past do not add -ed. They are often the verbs we use the most. They are 
irregular verbs: begin, bring, buy, do, eat, get, give, have, keep, leave, make, mean, put, see, sell, 
think, wear.
These verbs don’t change in the simple past: I (you/he/she/it/we/you/they) wore new shoes.
The verb be changes when used in the simple past: I was (you were, he/she/it was,  
we/you/they were) in the shoe store.

62 WRITING
We write an informational text to give readers new information. An informational text has a 
topic sentence that tells the reader the topic, or what the paragraph is about. Then there 
are at least three body sentences after the topic sentence. These sentences give facts and 
information, not the writer’s opinions.
The first soccer uniform had long, loose shorts. (fact)
The first soccer uniform was ugly. (opinion)
